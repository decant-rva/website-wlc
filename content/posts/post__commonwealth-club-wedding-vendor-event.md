# Taste of the Commonwealth Club - Lighting and Drape 2024

We were invited to the Taste of the Commonwealth event at the [Commonwealth Club](https://www.thecommonwealthclub.net/) to showcase our lighting, drape and decor in Richmond, Virginia.

Couples attending the event are able to see samples and meet with all types of local wedding vendors. Some of the vendors including [lighting and drape companies](/), chair and linen rental companies, vintage rentals, event caterers and more.

![Chandeliers and drape  backdrop cover the stage at the commonwealth club ballroom](wix:a16fb7_42c05227ab1945408ebacf7d05728593~mv2.jpg)

![Chandeliers and drape  backdrop cover the stage at the commonwealth club ballroom. Empire chanderliers hang from gooseneck chandelier stands and velvet drape lines the back of the stage.](wix:a16fb7_f619f009f5ad4d1dae7e0732738bf9b8~mv2.jpg)

Three of our ["Madeline" Empire Chandeliers](/product-page/madeline-empire-chandelier) hung from gooseneck chandelier stands with white covers. The back of the stage was lined witha luxurious champagne velvet-stretch drapery wall that perfectly matched the room's color palette.

The stage is the center point of the entire event and was sporadically occupied by a band. The chandeliers create an elegant centerpiece for the event and elevate the band's charm.

![sheer drape backdrop behind the photobooth at an event at the commonwealth club](wix:a16fb7_ac1cc6aaa3d54026b78adf967719db48~mv2.jpg)

![sheer drape archway over the event entrance at an event at the commonwealth club](wix:a16fb7_121ba2033b4c455fad6f7d4a379536fe~mv2.jpg)

The event entrance—there were plenty of drinks to go around! The entranceway looked gorgeous with our sheer drape touches. An elaborate drape archway is the first thing everyone sees. An ornate sheer backdrop is set up behind the photobooth to add a classy touch.

![](wix:a16fb7_6840b47b06a34ae3b13395800334602c~mv2.jpg)

We added a ton of [uplighting](/uplighting) all around the room. The best way to light up this space is to hit every column with uplights. No half-measures, it doesn't look as good.

For this event, the uplights were a bright warm white, but hundreds of other colors are possible. The most popular colors in the Commonwealth Club are this warm white, a candlelight amber, rose, and for party time: Blues and violets.

![](wix:a16fb7_e20aad4c0ef441aabcfac9d485098c3a~mv2.jpg)

The finishing touches. [String lights](/string-lighting) were hung from above the atrium to provide some vintage charm to the space. [White drop-down Edison bistro lights](/product-page/drop-down-edison-bistro-white) were the obvious choice for this space.

It was such a pleasure working alongside these fantastic vendors!
