Meet Richmond's favorite wedding decorators. The Wedding Lighting Co. team provides high end decor treatments for weddings & events in Richmond and the surrounding area.

Here's how Wedding Lighting Co. can help transform your venue to fit your vision.

## [Uplighting](/uplighting)

Uplighting is great for setting the ambiance of indoor receptions and enhancing outdoor events. Powerful wired and wireless uplighting solutions suitable for both indoor and outdoor settings are available.

## [String Lighting](/string-lighting)

Ideal for creating an inviting atmosphere, string lighting options include wedding bistro lights, twinkle lights, and pendants in various shapes, sizes, and colors. These lights are versatile for both indoor and outdoor events.

## [Pin Spots](/pin-spots)

Pin spots are perfect for illuminating specific areas, such as tables and centerpieces, without affecting the surrounding space. This technique is suitable for tall rooms, tents, and outdoor settings.

![](wix:a16fb7_72b38b848ae84e8785a874c9550972ca~mv2.jpg)

## Floral & Lighting Installations

Floral and lighting installations create breathtaking focal points that enhance the ambiance of a wedding venue, transforming ordinary spaces into magical settings. Suspended floral arrangements intertwined with twinkling fairy lights or elegant chandeliers add depth to ceilings, making the entire room feel lush and immersive.

Whether it's a grand floral chandelier over the dance floor or a delicate canopy of blooms and twinkling lights above the sweetheart table, these installations blend nature and illumination to create an unforgettable atmosphere that captivates guests and elevates the wedding’s aesthetic.

![Sarah Mattozzi Photography](wix:a16fb7_d5c804f98c6c4751a4270d9000a7d079~mv2.jpg)

Sarah Mattozzi Photography

## [Drapery Backdrops](/drape-walls-backdrops)

A drapery backdrop serves as an elegant focal point for a wedding ceremony, sweetheart table, or photo booth. These fabric installations create a soft, romantic atmosphere and can be customized in various colors, textures, and styles to match the wedding theme. Whether cascading in luxurious folds, layered with sheer and opaque fabrics, or accented with lighting and floral arrangements, drapery backdrops enhance the visual appeal of the space. They also provide a practical function by covering plain walls, dividing large venues, or framing important moments like the exchange of vows or the couple’s grand entrance.

![Kimie James Photography](wix:a16fb7_b88a32603b65472cb2f536e9bac839ac~mv2.jpg)

Kimie James Photography

## [Drapery Canopies](/drape-canopies)

A canopy of drape transforms a wedding space into a breathtakingly intimate setting by adorning the ceiling with soft, flowing fabric. Suspended from the center of the room or stretched across the entire venue, drapery canopies add depth and dimension while creating a luxurious, dreamy ambiance. They can be arranged in symmetrical swags, gathered elegantly at the center, or layered with twinkling lights for an enchanting effect.

![Anne Mark Photo](wix:a16fb7_658d4affc4394a08b3828bd90716f6c0~mv2.jpg)

Anne Mark Photo

## Draping Tent Legs

Draping tent legs is a stylish way to elevate the look of an outdoor wedding tent, turning a simple structure into an elegant and polished setting. By wrapping or swaging fabric around the tent poles, decorators can soften the industrial appearance of the framework while adding a touch of luxury. Flowy, sheer drapes create an airy, romantic effect, while heavier, gathered fabrics provide a more dramatic and formal look. Draping tent legs can also be paired with floral tiebacks, fairy lights, or greenery to seamlessly blend the structure into the overall wedding décor, ensuring that even the tent’s functional elements contribute to the beauty of the celebration.

## Disco Ball Displays

A disco ball display is a dazzling way to add a touch of glamour and excitement to a wedding celebration. Whether suspended above the dance floor or arranged in clusters throughout the venue, disco balls create a picture-friendly effect by reflecting light in countless sparkling patterns. Paired with strategic lighting, such as pin spots or colored LEDs, they can enhance the mood and elevate the energy of the reception. This setup is perfect for couples looking to infuse a retro, chic, or high-energy vibe into their special day. Disco balls can be combined with other elements, such as greenery, to enhance their charm.

## [Silk Florals & Trees](/catalog/silk-florals-catalog)

Using **silk florals** for wedding décor is a stunning and practical alternative to fresh flowers, offering lasting beauty and endless design possibilities. High-quality silk flowers mimic the look and feel of real blooms while providing durability, making them perfect for intricate installations such as floral arches, ceiling arrangements, and large centerpieces. They are also ideal for couples wanting out-of-season flowers or allergy-friendly décor without compromising on aesthetics. Silk florals can be repurposed throughout the event, from the ceremony to the reception, ensuring consistency in design and cost-effectiveness. Whether paired with greenery, drapery, or elegant lighting, silk florals bring timeless elegance to any wedding setting.
