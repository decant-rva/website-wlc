## Lighting & Drape can combine to transform a space

No matter where your heart takes your wedding design, there are decor and lighting rental options in Virginia that will meet your needs. Here are some of the best options for totally transforming your space.

## Twinkle Lighting Canopy

![Twinkle fairy lighting canopy christmas light ceiling in tent Richmond Virginia](wix:a16fb7_6150ffea79124935ae9e4716ce539d1f~mv2.jpg)

A twinkle light canopy makes this couple's private dance a moment that will live on in beautiful splendor. Crystal chandeliers sparkle in the lights.

Twinkle-lighting canopies are one of the best ways to elevate your event in Richmond. They can stand on their own or be added to tents and structures. They can serve as a canopy over the event area or line the walls to add even more to the design. The best part about using twinkle lights? They are impossible to miss. They add so much to the ambiance, in person, and in every single picture. The soft glow is easy on the eyes and adds magic to the affair.

## Paper Lanterns & Bistro Lights

![Richmond Wedding Decor for Prom](wix:a16fb7_e1bab31c3fa64b48b01eec10e2dadb6d~mv2.jpg)

This stunning themed looked perfectly matched the theme. Bistro lighting hung between the two balcony railings at the [Hotel John Marshall](https://www.jmballrooms.com/) in Richmond. Barrel-style paper lanterns are suspended from the bistro lights. Brilliant blue [uplighting](/uplighting) around the room completes the design.

This design was completed for a prom, but would definitely work for another type of events. A similar design with a few color changes can be used for a wedding or formal event.

![wedding lighting richmond john marshall](wix:a16fb7_9b1ac8df2def406595e107138141e774~mv2.jpg)

By just removing the lanterns, and changing the uplighting color, a totally new ambience is created. Lighting is such a powerful tool for transforming your space! These designs work well because they are intentional and thoughtful. The bistro lighting provides a classic focal point. Meanwhile, uplighting provides ambiences and sets the tone of the room.
