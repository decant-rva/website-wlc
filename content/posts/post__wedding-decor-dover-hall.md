# Wedding Decor - Dover Hall

Wedding decor at Dover Hall is a must in the spring season. Our team had the pleasure of setting up this beautiful event last year.

![Dover Hall. White drapery. Tall white curtains. wedding venue. Wedding decor.](wix:f5b740_98b89fe327d5475ea3c31d319c26b68f~mv2.jpg)

Our stunning white drapery serves as the backdrop to Dover Hall's elegant ballroom.

The curtains glimmer when the sun shines through, transforming the atmosphere into delicate and grandiose.

Romance. The sun sets, the room dims, warm lights mimic candlelight. Immaculate florals by [theflowerguyBron](https://www.theflowerguybron.com/about). Huge vertical arrangements and an impressive mantelpiece energize the room with color: soft pink, baby blue, cream and mauve.

![white drape transformation dover hall wedding decor](wix:f5b740_a1667ad93cd9405f94000319a08951c6~mv2.jpg)

As the wedding party gives speeches, the up-lights cascade along our elegant drapery providing an atmosphere of levity and reflection. Decor helps to solidify memories, bringing us back to this moment time and again.

![head table bridal party dover hall decor rentals](wix:f5b740_16be3193fd934903bf4127cdeaadeecf~mv2.jpg)

![father of the bride makes a speech during a wedding reception in Goochland Virginia](wix:f5b740_3c565d07d71a40869d7f6eeec8a58f43~mv2.jpg)

The speeches come to a close, the spotlight dims, and the room is aglow. Now our lights appear as an apricot tone, perfect for this spring wedding. The room seems to shift moods as the people in attendance go from one event to the next.

![Father Daughter dance dover hall](wix:f5b740_9df2d23796e1447b9f79fb7f1532c9d4~mv2.jpg)

It's party time! It's a "murder on the dance floor," and we're living for it. Our lights are more vibrant when accompanied by the violet hues from these glow wands. We curated a space with energy to get down and boogie. Shout out to DJ Rudy Lopez,who brought this party to life.

![the reception is a party at dover hall](wix:f5b740_7de54282cdbd4efeb7aec914ea33658b~mv2.jpg)

With one look, we created a vibe for every moment of the lucky couples wedding day.

For the grand finale, [cold sparks](/product-page/cold-sparks) to consecrate this wedding. The flames of passion shine bright as the newlyweds kiss in front of the Dover Hall Estate.
