# Lighting Rentals For Outdoor Events In Richmond

Aug 5, 2025

**Richmond loves an outdoor celebration**. River City plays host to weddings, fundraisers, concerts, and corporate gatherings in more than 300 for-hire venues; plus thousands of private spaces that turn into temporary event sites every year. Backyards and courtyards become grand ballrooms or cozy gathering spots with the right vision. Finding high-quality lighting rentals for outdoor events in Richmond is easy, and the right setup can completely transform your space.

## The highest-rated lighting rentals for outdoor lighting in Richmond

### [Waterproof Uplighting](/uplighting)

Waterproof uplights are the secret to lighting up trees, buildings, and tent walls without worrying about the weather. These lights are built to withstand rain and humidity, making them ideal for Richmond’s unpredictable outdoor events. Whether you're highlighting oak trees at Maymont or casting a warm glow on the side of a sailcloth tent, waterproof uplighting adds color, depth, and dimension to your space, all while staying safe and secure outdoors.

### [Bistro Lighting / Cafe Lighting](/string-lighting)

Bistro lighting, also known as café lighting, creates an instant sense of charm. Strings of warm white bulbs stretch across patios, courtyards, and backyards to bring a classic glow to the evening. These lights work beautifully for weddings, fundraisers, and private parties. We install them overhead between trees, poles, or structures to define the space and set the mood. Few lighting styles are as timeless and versatile as bistro lighting.

![Bistro string lighting for a wedding by the lake](wix:a16fb7_0d5e6d6ceb1e4febbe8e31814f50c24c~mv2.jpg)

### [Twinkle Lights](/product-page/icicle-twinkle-lights)

Twinkle lights add just the right amount of sparkle. Perfect for wrapping around trees, draping from beams, or weaving into greenery, these tiny bulbs create a soft, romantic effect that feels almost magical. Unlike bright string lights, twinkle lights create a subtler glow that shines best after dark. They’re a favorite for evening weddings, holiday events, and any space that could use a little extra shimmer.

### [Lighted Trees](/product-page/lighted-white-birch-tree)

Lighted trees make a stunning focal point at any outdoor event. Whether you’re outlining a few branches with soft lights or covering the whole canopy, lit trees bring elegance and atmosphere to gardens, driveways, and open lawns. We use dimmable, outdoor-rated lighting to create everything from a soft twinkle to a dramatic silhouette. It’s one of the easiest ways to make your event space feel layered, inviting, and custom-designed.

### [Glow Globes](/product-page/led-globe-light)

Glow globes bring a soft, ambient light to outdoor spaces.. These round, glowing orbs can be placed on the ground, floated in water, or suspended in trees for a sculptural lighting effect. They work well for modern weddings, garden parties, and any event that calls for something a little different. Available in warm white or custom colors, glow globes are as functional as they are beautiful.

### Richmond's Favorite Outdoor Event Venues

[**Linden Row Inn**](https://lindenrowinn.com/)

Linden Row Inn offers timeless charm right in the heart of downtown Richmond. The garden courtyard feels private and romantic, perfect for ceremonies with a historic backdrop and soft overhead lighting.

[**Virginia House**](https://virginiahistory.org/learn/virginia-house)

Set above the James River, Virginia House features formal English gardens and ivy-covered stone walls that feel straight out of a fairytale. It’s one of Richmond’s most elegant outdoor venues and pairs beautifully with classic uplighting and garden path illumination.

[**Historic Tuckahoe**](https://www.visithistorictuckahoe.com/)

The childhood home of Thomas Jefferson, Tuckahoe is full of historic Southern character with sweeping river views and wide open lawns. Tented receptions here shine with soft string lighting and tree-mounted uplights for a warm, welcoming glow.

[**Maymont**](https://maymont.org/visit/)

Maymont is one of Richmond’s most iconic outdoor spaces, with rolling hills, fountains, and manicured gardens that seem to go on forever. From the Italian Garden to the Japanese Garden, each spot is a stunning canvas for custom lighting.

[**The Branch Museum**](https://branchmuseum.org/)

Tucked away on Monument Avenue, The Branch Museum offers a gothic garden setting with dramatic architecture and an intimate courtyard space. It’s a favorite for couples who want a downtown venue that still feels green, quiet, and romantic after dark.
