# DANCE FLOOR LIGHTING

Our technicians often also provide lighting for clubs and venues, so we know a few things about dance floor lighting. Dance floor lighting is appropriate at many types of special events, and even some weddings. Versatile dance floor lighting packages come in many different sizes, and can fit into many special events and wedding’s lighting packages.

Basic dance floor lighting packages can be as simple as 2 lighting trees with 4 music-activated lights apiece. More advanced dance floor lighting packages can include LED wash lights, moving lights, and even professional lighting designers to operate the lighting to match the music.

With a lighting designer, even your uplighting around the room can be changed to suit the mood of the song.
