# DRAPE BACKDROPS & CANOPIES

Featuring a large and inclusive inventory of drapes for both room draping, canopy, and custom draping for weddings, meetings, and special events. Explore an inventory that includes large quantities of sheer(voile), polyester, poly-stretch, velvet, velour and shiny poly-knit. There are many drape options available:

- Drape backdrops
- Drape archways for doorways
- Drape cabanas
- Mundaps
- Chuppahs
- Drape canopies

All completed in any color and in many different fabric types. Custom drape backdrops are among the most popular services, and there many suggestions and wedding drape ideas in our [catalog.](/catalog)

[Drape canopies](/)for tents and ballrooms are usually custom-cut specifically for your event. We tailor each installation to fit the exact dimensions and design vision of your venue, whether it’s a romantic ballroom or an open-air tent. The fabric is measured, sewn, and rigged to create flowing lines, soft textures, and dramatic overhead visuals that elevate the entire space.

With options like sheer voile, satin, or rich velvets, and the ability to layer colors or add lighting elements, drape canopies transform plain structures into elegant, immersive environments. Work closely with an experienced team to ensure every canopy complements the theme and enhances the guest experience.

Combining drapes with other elements can create an even bigger impact. Drapes provide the perfect backdrop or framework for silk florals, allowing lush arrangements to cascade from ceiling swags or frame entrances and stages with elegance. When paired with chandeliers, drape canopies soften the light and add a sense of grandeur, creating a romantic, upscale atmosphere.

Uplighting can boost a drape look by casting warm or colorful glows onto the fabric, enhancing texture and depth while tying the overall color palette together. The result is a cohesive, dramatic design that turns any venue into a breathtaking event space.
