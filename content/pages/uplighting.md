# UPLIGHTING

Uplighting is one of the most popular lighting services for weddings and special events, and it’s easy to see why. These powerful LED fixtures are placed at the base of walls and aimed upward, creating dramatic visual effects and enhancing the overall atmosphere of a space. Uplighting allows planners and couples to shape both the look and feel of their event environment.

Because every event is different, the company offers a diverse selection of uplights, including wired, wireless, and outdoor options. Their wireless, battery-operated uplights can be controlled remotely from a central location, offering a sleek and cable-free lighting solution. With a battery life of 6 to 8 hours, these lights are both convenient and cost-effective.

For outdoor events, the company provides a wide range of durable, high-powered LED fixtures. These outdoor uplights are designed to withstand the elements and offer vibrant color choices that can become standout features of the overall design.

![Science Musuem of Virginia (10).JPG](wix:a16fb7_6964098a63ec44f2ba66ea27cbf061ef~mv2.jpg)

Uplighting doesn’t have to match the event’s color palette. In fact, one of the most popular options is a warm white, which adds a natural glow that flatters photography, enhances ambiance, and provides functional lighting without clashing with decor.

In events with a darker lighting scheme, uplighting can be paired with pin spots to highlight tables and key design elements while maintaining a moody, elegant atmosphere. String light curtains also work well in these settings, adding a gentle warm light that complements a low-light design.

Lighting impacts every aspect of an event. Whether the goal is to create a romantic wedding setting or an unforgettable special occasion, uplighting is a versatile and impactful place to start.
