# STAGES

We provide a simple solution to staging for your wedding or special event. Stages for that can fit both DJs and bands, at multiple different heights. Our wedding & event stages can be delivered alongside your lighting or drape, saving you on labor and delivery costs. Booking with us will make staging more affordable, so you can save budget for other elements of your event. We use 4 x 8 aluminum stage risers that can support most stage loads. Simple stage risers are easy to put together, and add little time to setup. Our locking stages pieces are unbelievable sturdy, and adjustable legs help make sure your stage stay completely level. Our stage risers have black, high-grip surfaces, and strong aluminum legs. For elegant events, carpets and stage skirts in many colors are available to make the staging a perfect fit for any theme.

Common stage sizes for bands are 12 x 16 for a 3-4 piece wedding band. We recommend at least 16 x 16 stages for 4-5 piece wedding bands. However, our risers can form all types of custom stages, including 4’ and 8’ runways stages for larger ceremonies and events. Stage risers can be elevated to 8”, 2’ or 3’ heights. 8” is standard for most wedding stages.
