# About

## THE WEDDING LIGHTING COMPANY

The Wedding Lighting Co. is a design & production company specializing in lighting, drape, decor, & AV designs & rentals for upscale events such as wedding receptions, fundraisers, and meetings. We work closely with planners, businesses, organizations, designers & individuals to craft beautiful ambiences and breath-taking decor treatments.

With a thoughtful, informative and organized approach, this part of your event planning process will be quick and easy. Quickly get up to speed on a considerable [portfolio of services](/services), or reach out directly to chat with an experienced designer who can answer your questions and help you make an informed decision.

# services

lighting, decor, drapes, audio, video, special fx, staging

# events

weddings, corporate events, galas, fundraisers, birthdays, mitzvahs, meetings, expos

# cities

richmond, charlottesville, fairfax, dc, williamsburg

# experience

10+ years in business

# vibe

professional, friendly, helpful, organized

# rentals

chandeliers, table lamps, led lights, specialty lights, speakers, drapes, tvs, video walls, projectors, stages, string lights

# team

20+ team members

# price

$1200 minimum
