### Transform Your Space with Drapery

Drapery is one of the most versatile and impactful elements in event design, offering endless possibilities to reshape and elevate any venue. Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a grand celebration, strategic draping creates atmosphere, defines spaces, and adds a layer of sophistication that truly transforms a room.

### Backdrops & Wall Coverage

###

Pipe and drape systems provide practical and beautiful solutions for dividing large spaces into intimate areas or concealing unsightly walls. These versatile installations can section off ceremony and reception areas, create VIP lounges, or simply provide a clean, elegant backdrop that enhances your event's aesthetic. Available in countless colors and fabrics, wall draping ensures every angle of your venue looks polished and intentional.

Backdrops for Stages & Head Tables

Create a stunning focal point with custom backdrops that frame your most important moments. Whether positioned behind a stage, sweetheart table, or head table, draped backdrops draw the eye and provide a refined setting for speeches, performances, and photographs. From sleek, modern panels to gathered, romantic styles, these installations ensure your key areas command attention with elegance.

### Ceiling Canopies

###

Many high-end weddings and events call for elaborate hanging drapery that completely reimagines a space. Drape canopies can span rooms in dozens of styles, from classic designs where fabric meets at a central point to parallel swags that flow across the ceiling. Recently, more creative drape designs have become popular, with asymmetric arrangements emerging as some of the most sought-after options. These ceiling treatments can be beautifully complemented with suspended chandeliers, string lighting, silk florals, or other decorative elements to create a truly breathtaking overhead display that turns any tent or ballroom into something extraordinary.

### Poly-Sheer

Sheer, or voile, is a popular fabric in the wedding and event industry. Drape panels are often times ordered at taller heights than your Uprights and puddled or pooled on the floor for a romantic, soft look. It is also used for up-lighting and creating layers of drape using Valance Hangers or Universal Clamps. as well as Tent Draping, Rafter Draping, and several other creative and unique applications. It’s very translucent, soft, and flows beautifully.

### Poly-Silk

Silk drapes are elegant fabric panels used to transform venues for weddings, galas, and special occasions. These lightweight, flowing textiles hang from ceiling structures or pipe-and-drape systems to define spaces, conceal areas, or serve as backdrops. While genuine silk is sometimes used for high-end events, most event drapes are made from silk-like polyester or chiffon fabrics that offer similar visual appeal with greater durability and lower cost.
