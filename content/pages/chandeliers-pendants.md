# CHANDELIERS & PENDANTS

Chandeliers are a popular addition to weddings and special events with all kinds of themes. We carry an ever-growing selection of chandeliers and can fit almost any vision. Part of the cost of event chandeliers comes from how difficult it is to safely transport complex crystal chandeliers with hundreds of pieces. We have developed a number of methods to safely transport our event chandeliers, extending the lifetime on our chandelier inventory. This means that our chandeliers are both affordable, and of high quality with no cosmetic issues. Our chandelier inventory covers most popular themes and there’s something that can fit into most events. Our collection of classic crystals chandeliers are great for formal, traditional weddings and events. Our modern crystal chandelier inventory can add elegance to events and weddings with a modern feel. Our collection of vintage event chandeliers are a perfect fit for your rustic or vintage wedding or event.

Most chandeliers we have in inventory have a number of customization options. These include: chain cover color, candle color, and your choice of bulb style. Chandeliers paired with some of our other services, such as uplighting, can create amazing looks that will be remembered by all of your guests.
