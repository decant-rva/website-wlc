# Flex Wireless Pin Wash

Much like our [Flex Wireless Pin Spot,](/product-page/flex-fuel-wireless-pin-spot) this handy light is designed for high-end events, this pin spot offers precision lighting to beautifully highlight your decor. While pin spots are designed for precisely lighting objects from across the room, these have a similar form factor while serving a more versatile role. Due to its larger beam angle when used as a pin spot, Flex Pin Wash can be used a lot closer to the table and still illuminate the entire thing. The brightness can be significant enough for face lighting for a band or speaker or wash lighting for a bar. All while maintaining a modern, sleek chassis.

- Dimensions 3.5W x 2.7L x 6.3H in Weight (lbs) 3.5 Color Silver
