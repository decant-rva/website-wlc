# Silk Italian Ruscus

Italian ruscus is one of the most versatile greenery options for wedding and event design, and these silk vines deliver the same trailing, layered look as the real thing without wilting through a long event day. Each strand runs 78 inches, giving our team enough length to drape across table centerpieces, weave through floral arrangements, or cascade down a ceremony arch with a natural, organic feel. The wire core runs through each vine, so individual branches can be shaped and positioned to fill space exactly as the design calls for rather than sitting flat. They hold their color and shape from setup through the end of the night, which matters on long event days where fresh greenery can start to droop or dry out under warm lighting. Three strands come per pack, and they pair cleanly with both fresh and silk florals across a wide range of color palettes and event styles.

- Dimensions 6'L - 30'L Weight (lbs) .1 Color Green
