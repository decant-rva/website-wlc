# Japanese Cherry Blossoms

Some of the best decor for a themed event. Known as sakura in Japan, cherry blossoms are a national obsession with deep cultural significance. They bloom in a mix of pink and white between late March and early April, symbolizing the transience of life and embodying renewal and hope. The Japanese celebrate the blooming with hanami, a springtime tradition of flower watching, picnics, tea ceremonies, and traditional performances. Our silk cherry blossoms are great on their own, or as an add-on to a string lighting concept. Individual cherry blossom hung from above are a great alternative to spending big bucks on real florals. They look great in person, and even better in pictures.
