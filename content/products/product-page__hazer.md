# Hazer

A hazer is a special effects machine designed to produce a thin, uniform atmospheric haze that enhances lighting effects and makes light beams more visible. It works by dispersing a fine, almost invisible mist into the air, creating a subtle and consistent haze that adds depth and dimension to the visual elements of an event. Unlike traditional fog machines, a hazer is designed to produce a longer-lasting effect without the dense cloud of fog, making it ideal for events, concerts, theatrical performances, and other productions where a controlled and persistent haze is desired.
