# Globe Bistro - Black

Also called cafe lighting or string lighting, we call it bistro lighting as it gives that classic bistro feel. Globe bistro is smaller and lighter weight than its Drop-down Edison counterpart. This gives us a lot of flexibility to hang it without bulky supports! The cords of black bistro lights look best against the night sky. We recommend them for outdoor freestanding bistro and against a dark ceiling or walls.

- Weight (lbs) 2.86 Bulbs LED
