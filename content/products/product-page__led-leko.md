# LED Leko

An **LED Leko** is a modern adaptation of the traditional ellipsoidal spotlight, commonly known as a "Leko." It's a powerful and versatile lighting fixture that offers precise control over the light beam's shape, size, and color. **Key features of an LED Leko include:**

- **LED light source:** Provides energy efficiency, long life, and a wide color spectrum.
- **Adjustable beam angle:** Allows for precise control of the light beam's size and shape.
- **Shutter system:** Enables the creation of various geometric shapes within the light beam.
- **Color mixing:** Offers a wide range of color options for creative lighting design.
- **Dimming capabilities:** Allows for smooth control of light intensity.

LED Lekos are widely used in theater, film, television, and live events to illuminate specific areas or subjects with high precision. They are essential tools for creating dramatic effects and enhancing visual storytelling.
