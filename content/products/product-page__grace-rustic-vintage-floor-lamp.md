# "Grace" Rustic Vintage Floor Lamp

This rustic vintage floor lamp comes in 3 different styles: brass, white, and gold. A great addition to any lounge area, dim cocktail area, casino setting, and more. Completely wireless, there are no limits to where these lamps can assist during your event. The fact that these are somewhat thematically neutral is another plus that makes them a versatile tool to add area lighting to your event. Our clients will often use these in "off to the side" areas, such as food areas, coat checks, and walk-ups, to replace harsh florescent or LED venue lights. Work these floor lamps into your decor plan and be glad you did!

- Dimensions 12" W x 12" D x 64" H Weight 10Lbs Bults 1 x E26
