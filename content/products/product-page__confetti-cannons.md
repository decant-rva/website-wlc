# Confetti Cannons

Confetti is a festive addition to events and celebrations, adding a touch of joy and excitement to the atmosphere. Typically made of thin paper or metallic material, confetti comes in a variety of shapes and sizes, from tiny squares to larger shapes like hearts or stars. Confetti creates a vibrant and dynamic visual effect when scattered or launched into the air, making it a popular choice for parties, weddings, parades, and other special occasions. Confetti cannons can be operated remotely, or be programmed to shoot at a precise time.
