# Chauvet Rogue R2x

The Chauvet Rogue R2X is a moving yoke wash light built around 19 RGBW quad-color LEDs at 25W each, producing 10,300 lumens of output with smooth, even color across the entire beam. A motorized zoom spanning 12° to 49° gives our team the range to dial in tight, punchy coverage or open up to a wide wash depending on the size of your space and what it calls for. Five independent LED zones allow for pixel-mapped effects, meaning the fixture can do more than a simple color wash — it can create movement and dimension across walls, drapes, ceilings, or dance floors. Pan and tilt ranges of 540° and 230° respectively mean the light can reach virtually any position in a room, and 16-bit dimming keeps color transitions smooth rather than stepped. Whether it's covering a large reception space with saturated color or adding movement to a stage backdrop, this fixture handles large-scale work that smaller lights cannot.
