# ADJ Jolt

**American DJ's Jolt** series encompasses a range of LED lighting fixtures designed to deliver high-impact visual effects. Known for their versatility and performance, Jolt products are famous in clubs, live events, and mobile setups. While there are various Jolt models, they generally share common characteristics:

- **High-powered LEDs:** Provide intense brightness and vibrant colors.
- **Strobe effects:** Deliver powerful, attention-grabbing visual impacts.
- **Multiple color options:** Offer a wide range of color choices for customization.
- **Compact design:** Makes them easy to transport and set up.
- **Versatile applications:** Suitable for various lighting scenarios.

Specific models within the Jolt series may offer additional features like pixel control, beam effects, or IP rating for outdoor use.
