# Icicle Twinkle Lights

**Twinkle lights, or mini lights as they're often called, are versatile lighting staples for any event.** Their classic charm makes them a popular choice for creating ambiance. Black twinkle lights seamlessly blend into the night sky, making them ideal for adorning outdoor structures like canopies. For a cascading effect, icicle lights are a timeless option. These strings of lights, with varying lengths, create a magical and elegant display when hung from a central point - perfect for canopies and other overhead structures.

- Weight (lbs) 1.1 Bulbs LED
