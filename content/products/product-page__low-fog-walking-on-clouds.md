# Low Fog - Walking on Clouds

Low fog is a special effect that creates an enchanting atmosphere. Using dry ice or a professional fog machine, low-lying fog is produced by cooling the fog to create a ground-hugging mist that adds a dreamy quality to the wedding venue. This effect is often used for first dances, entrances, and other key moments, as the fog gently swirls around the feet of the newlyweds, creating a fairytale ambiance. The low fog effect is safe, non-toxic, and can be easily controlled to suit the specific needs of the wedding or event.
