# Globe Bistro - White

Also called cafe lighting or string lighting, we call it bistro lighting as it gives that classic bistro feel. Globe bistro is smaller and lighter weight than its Drop-down Edison counterpart. This gives us a lot of flexibility to hang it without bulky supports! The cords of white bistro lights will blend in well with a tent, so we recommend them for most tent setups that want simple, timeless lighting.

- Weight (lbs) 2.86 Bulbs LED
